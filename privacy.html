Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Who we are

Asena ("we," "us") is developing a women's wellness and cycle-aware fitness app. This policy covers the waitlist on this website only.

What we collect

When you join our waitlist, we collect the email address you provide, and, if given, your first name.

Why we collect it

We use this information to send you updates about Asena's development and launch. We don't use it for anything else, and we don't sell or share it with third parties for marketing purposes.

How we store it

Your information is stored and processed using HubSpot, our email and contact management provider. HubSpot processes this data on our behalf under its own data processing agreement. You can read HubSpot's privacy policy at hubspot.com/legal/privacy-policy.

Your rights

Under GDPR, you have the right to:

  • Access the data we hold about you
  • Correct inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Withdraw consent at any time

You can unsubscribe using the link in any email we send, or contact us directly (below) to have your data deleted.

How long we keep it

We keep your email for as long as you remain subscribed, or until you ask us to delete it.

Contact
